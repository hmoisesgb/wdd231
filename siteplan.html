Site Name

League Experience

League Experience is a website where I will share my experience playing League of Legends. The website will showcase my fifteen most-played champions while providing useful information retrieved from Riot Games' Data Dragon.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase my fifteen most-played League of Legends champions through an interactive website, and the experience I had with the game. Champion information, images, abilities, and statistics will be retrieved dynamically using Riot Games' Data Dragon service. Visitors will be able to browse champions, view detailed information, and save favorites.

Scenarios

  • Which champions does the site owner play the most?
  • Which champions are recommended for beginners?
  • What is the peak rank of the owner?
  • What abilities does each champion have?
