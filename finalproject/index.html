My League Experience

League of Legends has been one of the games I have spent the most time playing. Throughout my experience with the game, I have played different roles and experimented with many champions and playstyles.

League Experience is a personal collection of that experience. The website focuses on the fifteen champions I have played the most while also providing information about their abilities, roles, difficulty, and other characteristics.

My Journey

Peak Rank

My highest achievement in ranked League of Legends is the Master Rank. I achieved that rank on Season 13.

Favorite Role

The role I enjoy playing the most is ADC. I enjoy playing ranged champs and doing the most damage in a fight, something that I really like about the ADC is the thought process you have to have in order to position correctly in a teamfight, since ADCs are very fragile and easy to kill by design.
