MY JOURNEY THROUGH THE RIFT

My League Experience

League of Legends has been a major part of my gaming experience. Over time, I have improved my mechanics, learned different roles, and developed a playstyle focused on ranged champions and teamfight positioning.

My Competitive Journey

One of my biggest accomplishments in League of Legends was reaching Master rank during Season 13. Reaching that rank required consistency, improvement, and a better understanding of decision-making throughout each match.

Ranked play helped me improve not only mechanically, but also in areas such as positioning, map awareness, team coordination, and adapting to different situations during a game.

Why I Enjoy Playing ADC

ADC is the role I enjoy the most because I like playing ranged champions and contributing a large amount of damage during fights. The role also requires careful positioning because marksmen are usually vulnerable and can be eliminated quickly if they are caught out of position.

I especially enjoy the decision-making involved in teamfights. A good ADC has to balance dealing damage with staying safe, choosing the correct target, and knowing when to move forward or retreat.

My Playstyle

Positioning

I focus on maintaining a safe position while still dealing consistent damage during fights.

Ranged Champions

I enjoy champions that allow me to attack from range and reward good spacing and movement.

Teamfighting

Large teamfights are one of my favorite parts of the game because they require awareness, timing, and quick decision-making.
